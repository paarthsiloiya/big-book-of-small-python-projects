# Hacking Minigame - Fallout-Style Terminal Hacking

Welcome to the **Hacking Minigame**, inspired by the iconic terminal hacking sequences from the Fallout series! Break into computer systems by finding the correct password hidden among decoy words in simulated computer memory. With multiple difficulty levels and enhanced visual effects, this is the ultimate cyberpunk puzzle experience.

## How to Play

### Game Objective
Find the secret 7-letter password from a list of words displayed in computer memory. You have limited attempts based on your chosen difficulty level.

### Making Your Guess
- Enter one of the available passwords shown in the memory dump
- After each wrong guess, you'll see how many letters match in the correct positions
- Use this "likeness" clue to eliminate incorrect passwords and narrow your search
- Find the password before your attempts run out

## Difficulty Levels

Choose your challenge level when starting:

- **NOVICE**: 6 attempts, 15 words, includes position hints for matching letters
- **ADVANCED**: 4 attempts, 12 words, likeness clues only
- **EXPERT**: 3 attempts, 10 words, for experienced hackers
- **MASTER**: 2 attempts, 8 words, the ultimate challenge

- Position hints show which letter positions matched

## Strategy Tips

### Elimination Method
- Start with any word to get your first likeness score
- Eliminate words that couldn't produce that score
- Use each guess to narrow down possibilities systematically

### Pattern Recognition
- Look for common letter patterns between words
- Focus on words with similar letter structures
- Use high-scoring guesses to confirm letter positions
