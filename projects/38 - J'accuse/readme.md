# J'ACCUSE! - Detective Mystery Game

Welcome to **J'ACCUSE!**, a thrilling detective mystery where you must find the missing cat Zophie before time runs out! Use logic and deduction to identify liars, gather reliable clues, and solve the case in this engaging puzzle game inspired by classic mystery adventures.

## How to Play

### Game Objective
- **Find Zophie**: Locate the missing cat by questioning suspects
- **Identify Liars**: Some suspects always lie, others always tell the truth
- **Make Accusations**: You have limited accusations - use them wisely!
- **Beat the Clock**: Solve the mystery before your time expires

### Difficulty Levels
Choose your challenge level at the start:
- **EASY**: 10 minutes, 5 accusations - perfect for learning the ropes
- **NORMAL**: 5 minutes, 3 accusations - balanced challenge  
- **HARD**: 3 minutes, 2 accusations - for master detectives only

## Game Mechanics

### Investigation System
- **Travel by Taxi**: Visit different locations around the city
- **Question Suspects**: Each location has a person with an item
- **Gather Clues**: Ask about people, places, and items you've discovered
- **Track Information**: Previously visited locations show suspect and item info

### Clue Analysis
- **Truth vs Lies**: Honest suspects give accurate information, liars give false clues
- **Cross-Reference**: Compare clues from different sources to identify inconsistencies  
- **Deductive Logic**: Use contradictions to determine who is trustworthy
- **Zophie Clues**: Special clues about the cat's location from select suspects

## Strategy Tips

### Logical Deduction
- **Map Locations**: Keep track of who and what you find at each place
- **Compare Stories**: Look for contradictions between different suspects' claims
- **Verify Facts**: Check clues against your direct observations
- **Pattern Recognition**: Identify which suspects consistently give false information

### Time Management  
- **Efficient Travel**: Plan your route to maximize information gathering
- **Strategic Questions**: Focus on suspects and items that appear in multiple clues
- **Save Accusations**: Don't accuse until you're confident in your deduction
- **Prioritize Zophie**: Direct questions about the cat often provide crucial leads

## Visual Features
- **Enhanced Interface**: Clean layout with emojis and clear section dividers
- **Status Display**: Real-time countdown and accusation counter
- **Color Coding**: Visual indicators for different types of information
- **Progress Tracking**: See which locations you've visited at a glance

Perfect for puzzle enthusiasts and mystery lovers who enjoy logical reasoning and deductive challenges!
