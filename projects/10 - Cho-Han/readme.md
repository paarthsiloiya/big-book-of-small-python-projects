# Cho-Han - A Japanese Dice Betting Game

Experience the fast-paced thrill of **Cho-Han**, a traditional Japanese gambling game where you wager on whether the sum of two dice will be **even (Cho)** or **odd (Han)**.

## How to Play

1. You start with a purse of ¥5000.
2. Place a bet (any amount between 1 and your current purse).
3. Guess the outcome: **Cho** (even) or **Han** (odd).
4. The dealer rolls two dice and announces the result using Japanese numbers (ICHI, NI, SAN, SHI, GO, ROKU).
5. If your guess matches the parity of the total, you win the bet (minus a house fee). Otherwise, you lose the bet.

## Rules & Payouts

- Correct guess: You win your bet amount, but a **10% house fee** is deducted afterward.
- Incorrect guess: You lose your bet amount.
- You can quit anytime by entering 'q' when prompted for your bet.
- The game ends when your purse reaches zero.

## Example Round

```
You have ¥5000.
Enter your bet amount: 600
Enter your guess (cho[even]/han[odd]): cho

The dealer rolls the dice...
The dealer rolled SAN(3) and ROKU(6).
The total is 9.
You guessed cho, and the total is 9. You lose ¥600.
```
