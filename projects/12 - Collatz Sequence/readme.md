# Collatz Sequence

Explore the fascinating **Collatz Sequence**, also known as the 3n+1 problem - one of mathematics' most deceptively simple unsolved conjectures. Despite being easy to understand, no one has proven whether this sequence always reaches 1 for every positive integer!

## How It Works

The Collatz sequence follows two simple rules:

1. **If the number is even**: Divide it by 2
2. **If the number is odd**: Multiply by 3 and add 1
3. Repeat until you reach 1

No matter which positive integer you start with, the sequence *seems* to always eventually reach 1. But proving this for *all* numbers remains an open problem in mathematics!

## The Collatz Conjecture

The conjecture states that for **any positive integer**, following these rules will always eventually reach 1. 

Despite its simplicity, this problem has stumped mathematicians for decades. It's been verified for enormous numbers (up to 2^68 and beyond), but no general proof exists.

## Example Sequence

Starting with **n = 12**:

```
12 → 6 → 3 → 10 → 5 → 16 → 8 → 4 → 2 → 1
```
