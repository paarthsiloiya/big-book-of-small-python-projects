# Hungry Robots - Strategic Escape Game

Welcome to **Hungry Robots**, a thrilling maze escape game where you must outsmart relentless pursuing robots! Use strategy and cunning to survive as badly programmed robots chase you through a maze filled with walls and obstacles. Make them crash into each other to survive!

## How to Play

### Game Objective
- **Survive**: Avoid being caught by the hungry robots (◊)
- **Strategy**: Trick robots into crashing into walls, dead robots, or each other
- **Victory**: Eliminate all robots to win the game

### Controls
Use the keyboard to move in 8 directions around your position:
```
(Q) (W) (E)
(A) (S) (D)  
(Z) (X) (C)
```
- **S**: Stay in place (useful for making robots crash)
- **T**: Teleport to random safe location (limited uses: ⚡)
- **QUIT**: Exit the game

## Game Elements

### Visual Characters
- **♦ (Player)**: Your character - stay alive at all costs!
- **◊ (Robots)**: Hungry robots that move toward you each turn
- **✗ (Dead Robots)**: Crashed robots that block movement
- **█ (Walls)**: Solid barriers that robots crash into
- **· (Empty Space)**: Safe areas you can move through

### Robot Behavior
- **Direct Pursuit**: Robots always move straight toward your position
- **Collision Physics**: Robots crash when they hit walls or other robots
- **Poor Programming**: They can't navigate around obstacles intelligently
- **Predictable Movement**: Use this to set up chain reaction crashes

## Game Features

### Dynamic Maze
- **Random Layout**: Each game generates a unique maze with walls and obstacles
- **Multiple Hazards**: Dead robots from previous crashes become new obstacles
- **Strategic Positioning**: Use the environment to your advantage

### Progressive Challenge
- **Increasing Difficulty**: More robots mean more complex escape scenarios  
- **Limited Resources**: Finite teleports add strategic resource management
- **Quick Thinking**: Fast-paced action requires immediate tactical decisions

Perfect for strategy game enthusiasts who enjoy outsmarting AI opponents through clever positioning and tactical thinking!
