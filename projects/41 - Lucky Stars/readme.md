# Lucky Stars - Press Your Luck Dice Game

Welcome to **Lucky Stars**, an exciting multiplayer dice game where you roll for glory but risk losing everything! Inspired by Zombie Dice, this enhanced version features beautiful Unicode graphics, multiple difficulty levels, and strategic "press your luck" gameplay that keeps everyone on the edge of their seats.

## How to Play

### Game Objective
Roll dice to collect as many ⭐ stars as possible while avoiding 💀 skulls. The first player to reach the target score triggers the final round, and whoever has the most points wins!

### Turn Mechanics
1. **Roll 3 dice** from the cup (mix of Gold, Silver, and Bronze dice)
2. **Count results**: Stars add to your score, skulls threaten your turn, question marks stay for next roll
3. **Choose**: Keep your stars and end turn, or risk it all and roll again
4. **Bust risk**: Collect too many skulls and lose ALL stars from this turn!

### Dice Types & Probabilities
- **🥇 Gold Dice (6 in cup)**: High star chance - 3 stars, 2 questions, 1 skull
- **🥈 Silver Dice (4 in cup)**: Balanced risk/reward - 2 stars, 2 questions, 2 skulls  
- **🥉 Bronze Dice (3 in cup)**: High risk, high skull chance - 1 star, 2 questions, 3 skulls

## Difficulty Levels

Choose your challenge level:
- **EASY**: Target 10 points, bust at 4 skulls - perfect for learning
- **NORMAL**: Target 13 points, bust at 3 skulls - classic experience
- **HARD**: Target 16 points, bust at 2 skulls - expert challenge

## Strategy Tips

### Risk Management
- **Know your dice**: Gold dice are safer, Bronze dice are dangerous but get pulled early
- **Count the skulls**: Track how many skulls you've rolled - don't get too greedy
- **Question mark strategy**: Keeping question marks gives you fewer new dice but more control

### Competitive Play
- **Score tracking**: Watch other players' scores to gauge when to take bigger risks
- **End game timing**: When someone approaches the target, decide if you need to take bigger risks
- **Psychological pressure**: Sometimes stopping early puts pressure on opponents to take dangerous risks
