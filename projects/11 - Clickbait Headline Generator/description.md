# Clickbait Headline Generator

Welcome to the **Clickbait Headline Generator**, a hilarious program that creates those irresistible, over-the-top headlines you see all over social media! Generate dozens of sensationalized titles that parody modern internet journalism with phrases like "You Won't Believe..." and "Number 7 Will Shock You!"

## How It Works

1. Enter how many headlines you want to generate.
2. The program randomly selects from 11 different clickbait templates.
3. Each template fills in random words from curated lists (nouns, places, states, adjectives, verbs).
4. Get instant viral-worthy headlines ready to post!

## Headline Types

The generator includes 11 classic clickbait formulas:

1. **Are Millennials Killing**: "Are Millennials Killing the [Industry] Industry?"
2. **What You Don't Know**: "Without This [Noun], [Things] Could Kill You [When]"
3. **Big Companies Hate**: "Big Companies Hate [Them]! See How This [State] [Person] Invented a Cheaper [Thing]"
4. **You Won't Believe**: "You Won't Believe What This [State] [Noun] Found in [Their] [Place]"
5. **Don't Want You To Know**: "What [Group] Don't Want You To Know About [Topic]"
6. **Gift Ideas**: "[Number] Gift Ideas to Give Your [Noun] From [State]"
7. **Reasons Why**: "[Number] Reasons Why [Things] Are More Interesting Than You Think (Number [X] Will Surprise You!)"
8. **Job Automated**: "This [State] [Person] Didn't Think Robots Would Take [Their] Job. [They] [Was/Were] Wrong."
9. **One Weird Trick**: "This One Weird [Noun] Trick Will [Verb] Your [Things]!"
10. **Scientists Hate**: "[State] Scientists Hate This [Noun] Because of Its [Adjective] Effect on [Things]"
11. **Can You Pass**: "Can You Pass This [Noun] Quiz? Only [Percent]% of People Can!"
