# Carrot In a Box - A Game of Bluffing and Psychology

Welcome to **Carrot In a Box**, a hilarious two-player game of deception, psychology, and pure luck! Based on the classic comedy sketch, this game puts players in a battle of wits where one person knows the truth and the other must decide whether to trust them or not.

## How to Play

1. **Two Players**: Enter names for both players
2. **The Setup**: Two boxes appear - one RED, one GOLD
3. **The Reveal**: Player 1 gets to peek inside their box while Player 2 closes their eyes
4. **The Choice**: Player 1 sees if they have the carrot or not, then Player 2 decides whether to swap boxes
5. **The Winner**: Whoever ends up with the carrot wins!

## The Psychology Behind the Game

This simple game creates a fascinating psychological battle:

### Player 1's Dilemma
- **If they have the carrot**: Do they act excited and risk Player 2 wanting to swap? Or play it cool?
- **If they don't have the carrot**: Do they act disappointed to encourage a swap? Or pretend they have it?

### Player 2's Challenge
- **Reading Body Language**: Watch Player 1's reaction for clues
- **Risk Assessment**: Is it better to stick with what you have or take a chance?
- **Trust vs. Suspicion**: Can you believe Player 1's behavior, or are they bluffing?
