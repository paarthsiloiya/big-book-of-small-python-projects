# Powerball Lottery Simulator

A comprehensive lottery simulation that lets you experience the thrill of playing Powerball without losing real money, featuring detailed statistics, multiple play modes, and realistic prize calculations.

## Features

- **Realistic Powerball Rules**: 5 numbers (1-69) + Powerball (1-26)
- **Quick Pick Option**: Random number generation with "quick" or "q" command
- **Multiple Play Modes**: 
  - Fixed number of plays (1-10,000,000)
  - Automatic continuous play until jackpot or manual stop
- **Complete Prize Structure**: All 9 prize tiers with accurate payouts
- **Detailed Statistics**: Win breakdown, rates, odds, and financial tracking
- **Real-time Progress**: Live updates during large simulations
- **Enhanced User Experience**: Emojis, formatting, and clear visual feedback

## Prize Tiers

- **Jackpot**: 5 numbers + Powerball = $1,586,000,000
- **Second Prize**: 5 numbers = $1,000,000
- **Third Prize**: 4 numbers + Powerball = $50,000
- **Additional Tiers**: $100, $7, and $4 prizes for various combinations

## Usage Modes

### Manual Selection
Enter your own numbers and Powerball for personalized play

### Quick Pick
Type "quick" or "q" to randomly generate numbers

### Simulation Options
- **Fixed Play**: Specify exact number of tickets (1-10M)
- **Auto Play**: Continuous simulation until jackpot or interruption
- **Progress Tracking**: Real-time updates for large simulations

## Statistics Tracking

- Prize breakdown by tier
- Total games played and money spent/won
- Win rates and net loss calculations
- Odds information display
- Financial impact analysis

## Enhanced Features

- **Interrupt Handling**: Ctrl+C to stop auto-play safely
- **Error Prevention**: Input validation and exception handling
- **Visual Enhancements**: Emojis, formatting, and clear sections
- **Replay Option**: Continue playing with new numbers
- **Screen Management**: Clear screen between sessions
- **Currency Formatting**: Proper comma separation for large numbers
